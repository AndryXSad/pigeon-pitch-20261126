An entry for make.fun's "Pigeon Pitch, One Line"
(u-donrulezz-pigeon-pitch-one-line-af06c6cf).

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one fixed line — the
elevator pitch a city pigeon gives investors for its startup — to
`/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness, no input: the same bytes on
every run.

## The pitch

Investors, before drones there was us: 200 years of same-day delivery, no batteries, no permits — just me, a breadcrumb, and a guy who points and says 'that one'.

The line was picked by a local pairwise tournament over 16 original
candidates, judged against the bounty brief: most on-subject, funniest,
most original, most pigeon. It went 3-0 in the local Swiss tournament.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
